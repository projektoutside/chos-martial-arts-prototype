We may provide limited information to service providers only when needed to operate requested services, maintain security, or comply with law. Service providers may not use mobile information or messaging consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. You may ask us to correct or delete information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.
